## Sources used in these notes

- Dan Becker, [Machine Learning for Business Decision Optimization](https://www.wandb.courses/courses/decision-optimization): the [decision optimisation](/research/data-science/decision-optimisation) notes.
- Richard McElreath, [Statistical Rethinking](https://xcelab.net/rm/): the [Bayesian data analysis](/research/data-science/statistics/bayesian-data-analysis) note.
- Joel Grus, [Data Science from Scratch](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/data-science-from/9781492041122/): much of the [statistics](/research/data-science/statistics) notes and the [gradient descent](/research/data-science/maths/gradient-descent) note.
- Kaggle, [Geospatial Analysis](https://www.kaggle.com/learn/geospatial-analysis): the [geospatial](/research/data-science/tools/geospatial) notes.
- Christoph Molnar, [Interpretable Machine Learning](https://christophm.github.io/interpretable-ml-book/): background for the [interpretability](/research/data-science/interpretability) notes.

## To read

- Lewis Tunstall, Leandro von Werra and Thomas Wolf, [Natural Language Processing with Transformers](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/natural-language-processing/9781098136789/)
